# Checkpoint 2026-11-17: VitaMoon 1.0.0 "Nouvelle Lune"

The first version, after eleven pre-releases on 15 and 16 November
([history/betas.md](../history/betas.md)) and the first measurements on a Vita: what 1.0.0 adds to
the last pre-release, what the Vita showed, what is checked on a PC, and what is not verified yet.
The previous reports are kept as they were: [16 November](2026-11-16.md) (with the full test plan)
and [15 November](2026-11-15.md).

## Since 1.0.0-beta.11

- **The log is one dated file per run**, `ux0:data/vitamoon/logs/vitamoon-<YYYYMMDD-HHMMSS>.log`, the
  last 30 runs kept (it was `vitamoon.log` and the four runs before it, so a session's log was lost
  after five starts of the app). The old files are moved into `logs/` on the first start.
- **Statistics files**: the screen refresh count went back by 65536 every 18 min 13 s (`vblank` read
  -65476 twice in the 34-minute session); the Vita's counter has 16 bits. Only the files were wrong.

Nothing else changed. The video path is the one measured below: it has not changed since beta.9.

What they show:

- **Smoothness**: the Vita shows 94 to 98 % of the frames when the PC sends 60 a second; the rest is
  Wi-Fi jitter (two frames arriving in one refresh: one waits, about 1.5 repeated refreshes a second).
  How smooth a session looks depends mostly on how often the PC sends 60 frames, which depends on the
  game's scene (9 % to 83 % of the seconds below 55).
- **Delay from a frame's first packet to the screen**: 97 ms median in the 34-minute session at 60
  fps: receiving the frame about 2 ms, decoding about 4 ms, the hardware decoder holding 3.6 pictures
  about 60 ms (decoder catch-up already takes back what it has ready, about 1.7 pictures a second),
  showing it about 29 ms (waiting its
  turn in the *Smooth* queue, drawing, the next screen refresh).
- **Still screens**: when the PC sends about 20 frames a second, the decoder still holds 3 pictures,
  now 50 ms apart: about 170 ms (80 s at the start of the 34-minute session). Sunshine's
  `minimum_fps_target` set to 60 should keep 60 frames a second there; not tried yet.
- **Wi-Fi**: in the 34-minute session (signal 76 %), a dozen freezes of 0.4 to about 2.7 s, clustered
  near minutes 17 and 28, when whole frames never arrived; each ended with the next key frame. 565 of
  the 599 frames lost were in them.
- **Sound**: 3 to 10 short gaps a minute in every session (the low-latency buffer ran empty); its
  cushion stays at its 50 ms ceiling. Whether the sound arrives late from the network or the Vita
  falls behind is not known.

Compared with VitaRPS5 4.2.2 on the same Vita (Louis's sessions of 30 October to 1 November, 720p,
60 fps, 10 Mbps, PS5 on Ethernet): VitaRPS5 shows 43.7 to 45.7 of the 59.7 to 59.9 frames it receives
a second (73 to 76 %), its decoder holds about 3.9 pictures (about 69 ms) and its own probes add up to
about 100 ms from arrival to screen. Same order of delay, far fewer frames shown; the two count
freezes differently.

## What is checked on a PC

| Check | Result |
|---|---|
| Build for the Vita (VitaSDK, GCC 15.2) | builds; the remaining warnings are in code inherited from upstream |
| PC tests, AddressSanitizer + UBSan | 349 checks, all passing, among them the log's files (32 checks for the log) and the library, twinkle, moving sky and tap rules (54) |
| Interface preview, 18 scenarios × 3 languages and the 5 themes, AddressSanitizer + UBSan + leak check, GPU rules | no memory error, no undefined behaviour, no leak, no texture freed while the GPU may read it; every interface text fits (sample names longer than their box are cut with an ellipsis, as intended) |

## Not verified

- The delay from a button press to the picture (VitaRPS5 measures it; VitaMoon does not yet).
- The cause of the sound gaps.
- Sunshine's minimum FPS target at 60 on still screens.
- The *Paced* and *Lowest latency* profiles and the options one at a time: every session so far is
  *Smooth*.
- The menus of beta.11 (twinkling and moving sky, favourites, tap time) ran on a Vita on 16 November
  with no problem reported, without being checked one by one; the dated logs of 1.0.0 have not run on
  a Vita yet.
- Everything else listed as not verified [on 16 November](2026-11-16.md#not-verified).
