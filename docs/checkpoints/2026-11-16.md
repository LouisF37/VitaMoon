# Checkpoint 2026-11-16: VitaMoon 1.0.0-beta.11 "Nouvelle Lune"

State of the project before the first measurements on a Vita, one day after
[the first checkpoint](2026-11-15.md) (Lune 1.0.0-beta.1): what changed, what is checked, what is
not, and the full test plan for the console.

## What changed since 15 November

- **Identity**: named VitaMoon, own title ID `VMOON0001` and data folder `ux0:data/vitamoon`; Lune's
  folder is moved there on the first start. New logo, LiveArea and GitHub images.
- **New in the app**: automatic reconnection after a Wi-Fi drop or a sleep, in-game quick menu
  (L + R + SELECT), five colour themes, a log on every run, quit-and-start when another game runs on
  the PC, keyboard layout *Text, any PC layout*.
- **New look** (beta.3): lunar sky behind the menus, Poppins for titles and Inter for text, a moon that
  fills while a stream starts or reconnects. Beta.4 makes sure none of it is prepared or kept during
  a game.
- **Safety** (beta.5): a PC's name, which is also its folder's name, is checked before the folder is
  used or emptied; the list of PCs found on the network is bounded.
- **First test on a Vita** (beta.6): changing the theme froze the Vita; the old background was freed
  while the frame being drawn still used it. Fixed, and the PC preview now checks this rule of the
  GPU on every screen. A settings file that cannot be written no longer closes the app.
- **What the preview cannot see** (beta.7): pairing can be cancelled and no longer waits without end;
  the PC's reason for a failed pairing is shown; the stream's display thread has 256 KB of stack and
  no longer renders glyphs during play.
- **First test on a Vita, continued** (beta.8): Play in the library did nothing, since Lune beta.1.
  Fixed; a preview scenario now goes from opening the PC to Play.
- **Full review** (beta.9): every journey replayed on a PC through the real menus, connection code
  and libgamestream against an imitation of Sunshine 2026.914 (29 journeys), every module read,
  static analysis, leak check. Fixed: a game the PC refused stayed "running" for the Vita (Play then
  failed for ever); the library and the reconnection now follow what runs on the PC; a damaged mDNS
  packet could freeze the network search; the home screen's PC detection thread did not really stop
  (leak, shared state); the PS button could stay locked after a stream; memory leaks at each PC
  opened and each library visit; a failed start-up went on; statistics files accumulated.
- **Start of the app** (beta.10): a start-up screen from the first frame (the moon waxes while
  VitaMoon loads, becomes the mark and moves into the home screen's top bar) instead of two seconds
  of a black text screen; the network, controls and PCs load on their own thread while the fonts
  render; the decoder probe that delayed the first opening of Settings runs in the background.
- **Library, trackpad and sky** (beta.11): the library says when each game was last played, sorts by
  favourites (SELECT) then by last played, and shows a session's figures only as an option; the
  trackpad's tap follows Moonlight for Android (250 ms, 20 pixels) with the time adjustable (100 to
  250 ms); the menus' stars can twinkle (on) and turn around the moon (off; four speeds, both ways).
- **Repository**: the branch is `main`, from which CI publishes each version.

Every item, with its source: [CHANGELOG.md](../../CHANGELOG.md).

## Not verified

Only the start of the app, the theme change (it froze the Vita, fixed in beta.6) and opening the PC
(Play did nothing, fixed in beta.8) have run on a Vita so far. The journeys check what the Vita
asks the PC and what the menus do with the answers; not the video, the sound, the controls, the
real Wi-Fi, the timings or the sleep. Not verified yet: every item listed [on 15 November](2026-11-15.md#not-verified), and
since then the move of Lune's folder, reconnection after a real Wi-Fi drop or sleep, the quick menu
over a real stream, the log written to the memory card during play, the text keyboard layout with
Sunshine, the readability of the sky and the fonts on the Vita's screen, the Vita's FreeType drawing
the Inter (OpenType CFF) and Poppins files, and what the waiting moon adds to the start time.
Since beta.11: the twinkling and the moving sky on the Vita's screen (smoothness, what they cost the
menus), the new tap rule with a finger, and favourites kept across restarts.

## Test plan on the Vita

Same game, same place, 3 to 5 minutes per session, *Statistics while streaming* on. Each session
leaves `ux0:data/vitamoon/stats/session-<date>.csv` and `.txt`; each run, `ux0:data/vitamoon/vitamoon.log`.

1. **Install**: the VPK installs as a *VitaMoon* bubble next to Moonlight; the LiveArea shows the mark,
   `VITAMOON` and `v1.0.0-beta.11`; Moonlight still starts with its own PCs and settings.
2. **Coming from Lune** (if Lune beta.1 is installed and paired): VitaMoon starts with the PC already
   paired and the same settings; the log says `data moved from ux0:data/lune (Lune)`; the Lune bubble
   can then be deleted.
3. **Menus**: from the first frame, the sky and the waxing moon, which becomes the mark and moves with
   the name into the top bar; no black screen with text. In the log, note the `start-up:` line (first
   frame, each step, Home) and `decoder sizes probed in ... ms`; Settings opens at once the first time.
   The home screen shows VitaMoon, the version and the build; a warning if Wi-Fi power save
   or Bluetooth is on. In each of the five themes (*Settings > General > Theme*), in English, French and
   Spanish, the text stays readable over the sky; titles are in Poppins, the rest in Inter.
4. **Pairing**: search the network, type the code in Sunshine; the Vita appears under its nickname in
   Sunshine; the PC shows as paired after restarting VitaMoon. Before that, once: start a pairing,
   press O on the code screen (back within a second); start again at once: the message says a request
   is still waiting on the PC; cancel it on Sunshine's PIN page, then pair.
5. **Start a game**: the moon fills with the steps written under it and is full as the video starts.
   In the log, note `waiting moon: N pictures drawn in M ms` and `started in ... ms`.
6. **Profiles**, one session each: *Smooth*, *Paced*, *Lowest latency*, *Original*.
7. **One option at a time** from *Smooth*: *Wait for late packets* off then 30 ms; *Fast colour
   decoding* off; *Spread work over the cores* off; *Video packet size* 1392; *Read the controls in
   step with the Vita* off. Change the option from the pause (START + L + R) and accept the restart.
8. **In game**: L + R + SELECT opens the quick menu over the game (statistics on and off, keyboard,
   pause, quit asked twice); the game gets no button while it is open, nor the ones used to close it.
   Keyboard layout *Text, any PC layout*: accented letters typed into Notepad come out as typed, with a
   French AZERTY PC layout.
9. **Robustness**: pause and resume ten times; quit the game from the pause; quit it on the PC during a
   stream (no reconnection expected); turn the Wi-Fi off during a stream, then on again, and put the Vita
   to sleep and wake it up: "Connection lost", the moon filling over the minute of tries, then the same
   game again; Circle cancels. With another game running on the PC, start one from the library:
   VitaMoon offers to quit the first and starts the second.
10. **Touch**: in mouse mode, keep a finger on the screen while pausing (the click must be released on
    the PC); five fingers on the screen in tablet mode. In trackpad mode, quick taps click and a swipe
    moves the pointer at once; try *Settings > Touch > Tap to click* at 100 and 250 ms.
11. **Library and sky**: after a session, the game comes first with "Last played today at ..."; SELECT
    puts a star on another game, which moves first and stays first after a restart of VitaMoon. In
    *Settings > General*, *Moving sky* at each speed and both directions: the stars turn smoothly, the
    menus stay as fluid as with a still sky; *Twinkling stars* off: the stars stay still.
12. **Log**: after a stream, `vitamoon.log` holds the start-up lines, the PC's Sunshine version, the
    timed start stages, the video, audio and controller lines, one health line per minute and the end
    of the stream with its cause; after a restart of VitaMoon the previous log is `vitamoon.1.log`.

To send back: `vitamoon.log` (and `vitamoon.1.log` to `vitamoon.4.log` for earlier runs), the `.csv` and
`.txt` files, one sentence per session on the feel (stutter, input lag, sound), and any crash dump
(`ux0:data/psp2core-*.psp2dmp`).
